Why this approach scales better

1. Index once

Convert the detail collection into a Map keyed by birdId. This costs O(m) time and O(m) additional memory.

2. Traverse once

Map over the bird collection once and perform an average constant-time lookup for each bird.

3. Derive reactively

computed() tracks both source signals, remains lazy and memoized, and prevents manual synchronization of duplicate state.
